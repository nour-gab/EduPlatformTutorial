## Notes

- UI chapters are subjective. The chapter gives you the exact HTML
  and CSS, but if you want to tweak (e.g., make the hero text bigger,
  use a different mint shade), do it after the chapter is complete
  and committed. Don't tweak mid-step — it muddies the diff.
- The reference image's "Annette Black, 274 friends" sidebar widget
  is implemented in Ch.21, not here. Step 13.3's `_Layout.cshtml`
  includes the sidebar slot but the `ProfileSidebarViewComponent`
  itself returns an empty `<aside>` until Ch.21.
- If the colorful cards' banners don't show the right color, check
  that the `Category.ColorHex` is seeded correctly (Ch.05 §5.5's
  `SeedCategoriesAsync` uses `#F8BBD0` for pink, `#FFE0B2` for
  orange, `#D1C4E9` for purple, `#C8E6C9` for green).
- The `Skill: VLM` step (13.10) is optional. If you don't have a
  reference image handy, skip it and rely on visual inspection
  against your memory of the reference.
